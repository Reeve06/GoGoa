Our Heritage & Passion

Meet Head Chef Roy & Valentina

Founded by Head Chef Roy and Valentina, Go Goa Takeaway brings the vibrant coastal flavors of India's sunshine state directly to Scotland. Both raised in Vasco Da Gama, Goa, Roy spent over 20 years perfecting his craft across top establishments in Dubai, international cruise liners, the USA, and Scotland.

Goan cuisine is world-renowned for its 450-year Portuguese influence, creating a unique harmony between Catholic and Hindu cooking styles. Signature dishes rely on fresh coconut, toddy vinegar, dried Kokum fruit, and fragrant roasted spices.
